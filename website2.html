04 / about

i'm still figuring out what i'm becoming.

I don't think a degree, a job title or a list of skills tells you much about a person.

I'm interested in a lot of things. Technology. Finance. Design. Business. People. Ideas. Sometimes completely unrelated things that somehow become related six months later.

I like taking things apart — sometimes literally, sometimes intellectually — and understanding what makes them work.

And when something keeps bothering me, I usually end up trying to build something around it.
